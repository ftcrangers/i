Inside the machine

Meet the Rangers robot.

Every mechanism has a job. Our robot combines a drivetrain, scoring system, and sensors that support repeatable performance.

Drivetrain · Swyft Drives

Our drivetrain uses Swyft Drives, selected for a compact setup, speed, and durability.

Scoring hood

A shaped hood guides game pieces toward a more consistent scoring trajectory. We refine its angle and geometry through testing.

Limelight vision

The Limelight camera helps identify AprilTags for alignment and can support game-piece color detection.

Odometry & pathing

Odometry pods provide movement measurements that support autonomous navigation and pathing routines.

CAD & build

From first sketch to field-ready.

We use CAD and an iterative engineering process to make design decisions, test ideas, and improve reliability.

01

Find the problem

Define the challenge and identify what the robot needs to do consistently.

02

Design & prototype

Compare mechanisms, use CAD, and turn promising ideas into working prototypes.

03

Test & improve

Run match-like tests, collect feedback, and refine the robot for repeatable performance.
